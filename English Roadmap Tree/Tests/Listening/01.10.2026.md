# First listening
rules:
```
Сейчас только **первое прослушивание — gist**.

Не смотри transcript. Не ставь на паузу. Скорость обычная. Preparation пока тоже пропусти — хочу сначала увидеть чистое понимание речи.

После одного прослушивания ответь **по-английски**, как умеешь:

1. Who are the two people?
   They are students. They meeted in software engineering class.
2. What is happening?
   The boy was late to the lesson. He asked the girl sit with her and took any milions questions about everything in the room.
3. What does one student want to know?
   He wanted to know what page he should open, when the teacher want take a control work and how theme named.
4. What is the main problem in the conversation?
   He choose wrong class. He should to visit the history class but him visit software engineering class.
5. **How much did you understand?** — примерно `60% / 70% / 80% / ...`
   80-90%
```

Исправления:
1) They are students. They <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">meeted</mark> in <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;"> </mark> software engineering class. - ошибка.
   meet - это неправильный глагол, в прошедшем **met** и пропущен артикль **a**
2) The boy was late to the lesson. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">He asked the girl sit with her and took any milions questions about everything in the room</mark>
   He asked the girl if he could sit with her and asked her lots of questions about the class.
   `ask somebody + to do` - попросить кого-то сделать что-то. Но парень сам хотел сесть с девушкой рядом. **Asked if he could sit with her**.
3) He wanted to know what page he should open, when the teacher want take a control work and how theme named
4) He was supposed to be(он должен был быть) in a history class, but he went to a software engineering class.

```

1. How late was the student?

5. Why does the student become confused when the teacher talks about SEO?
```

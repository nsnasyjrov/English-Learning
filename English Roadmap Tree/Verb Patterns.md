- `verb + to-infinitive` → `promise to do`, `want to do`, `decide to do`
- `verb + V-ing` → `suggest doing`, `enjoy doing`, `finish doing`
- `verb + preposition + object` → `reply to someone`, `wait for something`, `depend on something`
- `verb + object` → `call someone`, `discuss something`, `answer a question`

# verb + to + infinitive

`He promised to check it`

`He suggested to look it`

`She promised to call me later`

`She promised to reply to me later`
